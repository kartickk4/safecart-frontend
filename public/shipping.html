Multi-Carrier Shipping Protocol

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Logistics Aggregation & Courier Networks

SecureKaro operates as a multi-carrier logistics aggregation platform. We aggregate premier national logistics carriers — including Delhivery, Shadowfax, DTDC, Shiprocket, and NimbusPost — providing comprehensive shipping coverage to more than 29,000+ pin codes across India.

When a merchant books a shipment on SecureKaro, the platform compares live freight tariffs, transit estimates, and performance ratings across integrated carrier networks to select the optimal courier service for the package.

2. Package Pickup & Dispatch Protocol

Once a shipping booking is confirmed and the automated Air Waybill (AWB) label is generated:

  • Doorstep Pickup Scheduling: The courier partner is automatically assigned and dispatched to the merchant's specified pickup address.
  • Pickup Turnaround Time: Pickups are executed within 24 hours of booking confirmation (Monday through Saturday, excluding national holidays).
  • Digital Manifest Scanning: Handover is verified by the courier executive scanning the barcoded AWB shipping label on the package.
3. Standard Transit Timelines Across India

Delivery transit timelines vary based on shipping zone and destination geographic category:

Shipping Zone Coverage Region Estimated Transit Time
Zone A (Intracity / Local) Deliveries within the same metropolitan area or district 1 – 2 Business Days
Zone B (Regional / State) Deliveries within the same state / adjacent zone 2 – 3 Business Days
Zone C & D (Metro-to-Metro / National) Major commercial hubs (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai) 2 – 4 Business Days
Zone E (Special & Remote) North-East states, Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, islands 4 – 7 Business Days

*Timelines may be subject to minor variations during national festive periods, severe weather advisories, or regional transport restrictions.

4. Real-Time Tracking & Shipment Milestone Alerts

Every parcel dispatched through SecureKaro is assigned a unique Air Waybill (AWB) tracking number:

  • Public Tracking Portal: Both merchant and recipient can view live GPS transit milestones 24/7 at securekaro.in/track.
  • Automated SMS Notifications: SMS milestone updates are triggered upon pickup, out-for-delivery arrival, and final delivery completion.
5. Delivery Attempts & Non-Delivery (NDR) Management

To minimize RTO and ensure successful first-attempt deliveries:

  • Couriers make up to 3 consecutive doorstep delivery attempts.
  • In the event of consignee unavailability or incorrect address, an automated Non-Delivery Report (NDR) workflow allows the merchant or recipient to reschedule delivery or provide updated directions.
  • If the parcel remains undelivered after 3 attempts, it is safely returned to origin (RTO) to the merchant's warehouse.
6. Cash on Delivery (COD) Processing

For COD shipments, our carrier partner collects the exact invoice cash amount upon package handover. The collected funds are reconciled through automated electronic feeds and deposited directly to the merchant's bank account within T+2 to T+4 business days.
