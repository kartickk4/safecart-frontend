2. Categories of Personal Data Collected

As a logistics aggregator and courier shipping platform, SecureKaro collects only the minimum data required to fulfill shipping bookings and generate delivery waybills:

  • Account & Merchant Credentials: Full name, trade entity name, email address, and mobile number verified via OTP.
  • Consignor & Consignee Logistics Details: Pickup address, recipient delivery address, landmark, PIN code, and recipient telephone number for delivery coordination.
  • Shipment Information: Parcel dead weight, physical package dimensions, declared item value, and invoice numbers for generating compliant carrier shipping manifests.
  • Financial & Settlement Data: Merchant bank account numbers, IFSC codes, and UPI IDs collected strictly to disburse settled COD collections and wallet refunds. Online payments are processed directly by RBI-authorized payment aggregators (Cashfree); SecureKaro never stores card CVVs or passwords.
3. Specified Lawful Purpose of Data Processing

Personal data is processed strictly for legitimate logistics and shipping management purposes:

  • Calculating live carrier shipping rates across Delhivery, Shadowfax, DTDC, Shiprocket, and NimbusPost.
  • Generating barcoded AWB shipping labels and scheduling carrier doorstep pickups.
  • Transmitting live SMS tracking alerts and delivery milestone updates.
  • Reconciling and remitting verified COD amounts to merchants.
  • Complying with statutory GST and e-Way bill legal mandates.
4. Third-Party Data Processors & Safeguards

SecureKaro never sells, rents, or monetizes user personal data. Data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:

  • Licensed Courier Partners: (Delhivery, Shadowfax, Shiprocket, DTDC) to execute physical package transit and delivery.
  • Payment Aggregators: (Cashfree Payments India Pvt. Ltd.) to securely process online wallet recharges and bank account payouts.
  • Privacy Masking: Consignee phone numbers are partially redacted on public tracking web pages (e.g., +91 ******1234) to prevent unauthorized automated data harvesting.
5. Data Principal Rights under Chapter III, DPDP Act, 2023

Every user (Data Principal) has the following statutory legal rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of your personal data processed by SecureKaro.
  • Right to Correction: Update or rectify incomplete or inaccurate contact, address, or payout information anytime via your profile.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Request permanent account deletion and erasure of your personal data by writing to logistic@securekaro.in. Requests are fulfilled within 72 hours, subject to statutory tax retention rules.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Direct recourse to our Nodal Grievance Redressal Officer.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate an individual to exercise data rights in the event of death or incapacity.
6. Data Security & Hosting within India

All application databases, logs, and transaction records are hosted on secure enterprise cloud infrastructure located strictly within the territory of India. Security protections include 256-bit SSL/TLS end-to-end data encryption, salted bcrypt password hashing, role-based access control, and automated intrusion detection.
