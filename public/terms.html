2. Courier Partners & Physical Carriage of Goods

Physical carriage, sorting, transportation, and doorstep delivery of parcels are executed by licensed third-party express logistics carriers, including but not limited to Delhivery, Shadowfax, DTDC, Shiprocket, and NimbusPost ("Courier Partners").

  • SecureKaro acts as a technology intermediary between merchants and Courier Partners.
  • Transit schedules, pickup routes, and delivery attempts are governed by the respective operational guidelines of the selected Courier Partner.
  • SecureKaro provides automated live tracking and non-delivery reports (NDR) to facilitate seamless parcel journey resolution.
3. User Account, Verification & KYC Compliance

To use SecureKaro courier booking services, merchants must register with a valid mobile number verified via One-Time Password (OTP). In accordance with commercial carriage regulations:

  • Merchants must provide accurate pickup address, GSTIN (if registered), and legal trade details.
  • Users must strictly refrain from shipping prohibited, hazardous, flammable, perishable, illegal, or contraband items under the Indian Carriage by Road Act, 2007 and Indian Post Office Act.
  • Merchants are responsible for accurately declaring parcel dead weight, volumetric dimensions (Length x Breadth x Height), and package value.
4. Shipping Tariffs, Wallet & Payment Terms

Shipping tariffs are displayed in Indian National Rupees (INR ₹) based on pickup and destination pin codes, zone classification, and billable weight:

  • Prepaid Courier Wallet: Merchants maintain an active prepaid wallet balance to pay for booking freight charges and label generation.
  • Online Payment Gateways: All wallet recharges and payments are processed securely through RBI-authorized payment aggregators (such as Cashfree Payments India Pvt. Ltd.). SecureKaro never stores customer card numbers or net banking credentials.
  • Weight Discrepancy Adjustments: If a Courier Partner's automated weighing hub detects higher dead weight or volumetric weight than declared, the verified freight difference is adjusted from the merchant's wallet balance.
5. Cash on Delivery (COD) Collection & Remittance

For Cash on Delivery (COD) shipments booked through SecureKaro:

  • The Courier Partner collects the specified COD cash amount directly from the consignee at the time of doorstep parcel delivery.
  • Collected COD proceeds are reconciled with the Courier Partner and remitted directly to the merchant's registered bank account via automated IMPS/NEFT/UPI within the standard courier settlement cycle (typically T+2 to T+4 business days).
  • SecureKaro does not charge any hidden deduction on the merchant's principal COD collection.
