Transparent Cancellation & Refund Framework

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 2026

1. Shipment Booking Cancellations

At SecureKaro, we provide instant flexibility for merchants and shippers to manage their courier bookings:

✅ Cancellation Before Courier Pickup
If an order is cancelled prior to physical package pickup by the courier executive, 100% of the courier freight charges and fees are immediately refunded back to the merchant's prepaid wallet balance with zero cancellation fee.
⚠️ Cancellation After Courier Pickup
Once a parcel is physically picked up and manifested into the carrier's sorting hub, transit cannot be cancelled in flight. The shipment must be marked for Return to Origin (RTO), and return freight charges apply as per the courier partner's tariff rate.
2. Wallet Balance Recharges & Refund to Source

SecureKaro operates a prepaid wallet for automated freight fee deductions. If a user wishes to close their account or withdraw unutilized wallet balance:

  • Withdrawal to Bank Account: Users can request a wallet withdrawal directly from the Wallet console to their verified bank account or UPI ID.
  • Source Payment Refund: If an erroneous or duplicate online wallet recharge occurred via Payment Gateway (Cashfree), the user may email logistic@securekaro.in with the transaction ID. Unspent funds will be reversed to the original payment source within 5 to 7 business days.
  • No hidden processing penalties are deducted on legitimate wallet balance refunds.
3. Undelivered Shipments & Return to Origin (RTO)

In the event that a shipment cannot be delivered due to consignee unavailability, incorrect address, or buyer refusal:

  • Our automated NDR (Non-Delivery Report) system triggers automated SMS and IVR verification to attempt re-delivery (up to 3 attempts).
  • If the delivery cannot be completed, the parcel is flagged as RTO and securely routed back to the merchant's pickup warehouse.
  • Real-time GPS reverse tracking is visible throughout the return journey.
4. Lost or Damaged Parcel Transit Claims

If a parcel is confirmed lost or physically damaged during carrier transit:

  • The merchant must file a support ticket within 48 hours of delivery or non-delivery notice, including clear package photographs or proof of value.
  • SecureKaro initiates a joint inquiry with the carrier partner (Delhivery, Shadowfax, DTDC, etc.).
  • Upon carrier investigation and verification, approved compensation or freight refunds are credited to the merchant's account within 10 to 14 business days.
5. Refund Processing Timelines

All approved refunds follow these strict banking turnaround times:

  • Wallet Credit: Instantaneous (under 5 minutes).
  • UPI / IMPS Bank Transfer: 24 to 48 hours.
  • Credit/Debit Card / Net Banking Reversal: 5 to 7 business days depending on the issuing bank's settlement cycle.
